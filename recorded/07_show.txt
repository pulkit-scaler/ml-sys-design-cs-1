Background traffic at 20 requests a second, so the index is busy. About 10 seconds at each setting.
  nprobe  128   mean ANN search  1.35 ms over 143 searches   candidates hop p50   6.0 ms
  nprobe 1024   mean ANN search  2.51 ms over 141 searches   candidates hop p50   6.8 ms
  nprobe put back to 128
  PASS  8x the clusters makes each search 1.9x slower under load (1.35 -> 2.51 ms)
  note  the whole candidates hop moves less, and noisily, because most of it is moving data, as in trace

TEACHING POINT  A few times slower, not 8x, since each search has a fixed cost. More recall is still paid for in latency on every request, and at 100x the catalogue that cost no longer fits in 30 ms, which is Checkpoint 9.1.
